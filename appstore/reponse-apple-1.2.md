## (1) Réponse à l'équipe de vérification

Hello,

Thank you for the review. We would like to respectfully ask for a second look at this submission, because we believe Guideline 4.3(b) was applied to a very different app than the one you reviewed.

**This is a corrective update to an app that is already on the App Store.** Prout Box 1.0 was approved on October 28, 2026 and 1.1 on November 1, 2026 (both with the same concept, the same name and the same main screen). Version 1.2 (build 7) fixes a bug that prevents recording on iOS (an audio session issue), adds a back button on every screen and the layout fixes for small and large iPhones. Rejecting the fix leaves the live version 1.1 broken for our users, which hurts the App Store experience rather than protecting it.

**The app is not "primarily a fart app": the sound is the starting point of a social game.** What users actually spend their time on:

- **Recording and a comic jury** — the user records their own sound, a three-judge jury (Mamie Josette, Pr. Von Pfft, DJ Flatule) scores it on power, duration, bursts and groove, and an analyzer computes a score out of 10,000 with absurd statistics.
- **A worldwide ranking by country** ("TOP PROUTS") where people listen to and rate each other's recordings from 1 to 5, with a Bayesian average, reporting, automatic hiding and manual moderation by the developer before anything goes live.
- **Private groups with mini-games** — friends join with a 6-letter code, share a private gallery, play "Qui a pété ?" (guess who made the sound), a daily themed challenge and "the imitator" (reproduce a reference sound, similarity measured by the analyzer), with a leaderboard of the best "noses".
- **Missions, medals and titles** — 27 medals with rarities, 12 secret medals, daily challenges, a progression of titles.
- **Moderation** that goes beyond the guidelines for user-generated content: pre-approval of every public recording, voice detection to refuse speech, report button, automatic hiding, deletion by the author, banned-word list.

The six synthesized sounds on the main screen are the "instrument"; the game, the jury, the rankings and the groups are the product. We believe this distinguishes Prout Box from the saturated category the guideline targets, exactly as the guideline allows ("may include features or characteristics that distinguish it").

We would be grateful if the update could be reviewed again with these features in mind. We are happy to provide a demo: on the main screen, tap the title "Prout Box" five times to generate four recordings and see the jury, then open TOP PROUTS and GROUPES. No account is needed.

Thank you for your time and your help.

Noa Madar — Amuï studio

## (2) Appel (App Review Board)

**App:** Prout Box (Apple ID 6827309393), iOS 1.2 (7), submission 64f56121-4d29-40c6-83f9-50bae74a8c45
**Guideline cited:** 4.3(b) Design – Spam

We are appealing the rejection of a corrective update to an app that the App Review team approved twice in the previous week (1.0 on October 28, 2026 and 1.1 on November 1, 2026), with the same concept and the same name. Version 1.2 fixes a recording bug present in the live version; the rejection leaves version 1.1 on the App Store with a broken feature.

We do not believe Prout Box falls under 4.3(b). The guideline targets apps that only play a sound. Prout Box is a social game built around user recordings: a comic jury that scores each recording, a worldwide ranking by country with ratings and moderation, private groups with three mini-games ("guess who did it", a daily themed challenge, an imitation challenge measured by an audio analyzer), missions, 39 medals and a progression of titles, plus pre-approval of every public recording, voice detection, reporting and author deletion. The sound is the instrument; the game and the community are the product.

We would appreciate a review of this decision, or guidance on what would make the update acceptable, since the concept itself was accepted twice. We are not resubmitting the build until we hear back, as requested in the rejection message.

Thank you,
Noa Madar — Amuï studio
