Frequent questions

The microphone does not work

Check that Prout Box is allowed to use the microphone: Settings › Prout Box › Microphone. If you refused the first time, that is where to change it. Also update the app: version 1.2 fixed a bug that blocked recording.

Nothing happens when I flip the phone

Tap the box once first: iOS then asks for access to the motion sensor. Then flip the phone firmly, screen down.

No sound in silent mode

The app plays sound even in silent mode. If you hear nothing, turn the volume up with the side buttons and check that no Bluetooth headset is connected.

How do I change the language?

Tap the round button at the top left of the main screen (FR, EN, ES). The app follows your phone's language the first time.

My entry does not show up in the ranking

Each entry is checked before it appears, usually within a quarter of an hour. Open TOP TOOTS › ME to see whether it is in review, online or rejected. Voices are refused.

How do I remove something I published?

TOP TOOTS › ME or your country › tap your entry › DELETE (twice to confirm). In a group: GALLERY › your entry › REMOVE. If you changed phone, write to us with your name in the ranking, the country and the date, and we will remove it.

I want to report an entry

Tap the entry, then REPORT (twice). In the public ranking it is hidden for everyone after three reports; in a group it disappears immediately.

Delete all my data

Uninstalling the app erases everything on the phone. For what you published online, remove it from the app or write to us: we delete it, files included.
