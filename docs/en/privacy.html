Who is responsible

Prout Box is published by SAS NOMAD, France (designed and developed by Noa Madar). For any question or request about your data: contact@proutbox.com.

In one sentence: Prout Box asks for no account and no e-mail. Your recordings stay on your phone, except the ones you choose to publish in the ranking or send to a group.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your audio recordings are stored on your device. You can delete them at any time in the app, or by uninstalling it.
  • Your preferences (last sound chosen, the name you picked, country, language, medals) are kept on the device.
  • A random identifier is created on your device. It contains no personal information; it is used to count one vote per person, to find your own published recordings and to record your order of arrival among players (the “Pioneer” medal).

What is sent online, if you publish

When you tap “Publish”, and only then, we send to our servers (Supabase, hosted in the European Union):

  • the audio recording (6 seconds at most);
  • the name you chose (2 to 12 characters), the country you selected and the jury score;
  • the random identifier of your device;
  • your city, if you accept: when publishing, the app may ask for your location to show your city next to your entry. The position is rounded to about 1 km before being converted to a city name by the BigDataCloud service, and only the city name is kept, never your coordinates. You can refuse the permission or remove the city with one tap before publishing.

A published recording can be listened to and rated by every user of the app. You can remove it at any time (“Delete” in the ranking): it is then erased from our servers, file included. The ratings you give are also stored, linked to your device identifier.

Groups

You can create a private group with your friends (6-letter code). In a group you choose a nickname, and the recordings you send there, your ratings and your answers to the “Who tooted?” game are visible to the members of that group only. They are stored on our servers while you are a member; you can remove a recording or leave the group at any time. When the last member leaves, the group and its content are deleted.

Moderation

Every recording published in the public ranking is checked before it becomes visible: an automated check analyses the recording you published in order to refuse voices, and doubtful cases are listened to by a moderator. This check is run by an automation service (GitHub Actions) which downloads the published recording for the time of the analysis and does not keep it. Names go through a list of forbidden words. Every entry can be reported; an entry reported several times is hidden automatically.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you record, at your request (6 seconds at most).
  • Motion: only to detect that you flip the phone.
  • Location (optional, “while using”): only when publishing, to find your city. Never in the background.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-up, no background location.
  • No advertising, no tracker, no usage analytics.
  • No data sold or passed on to third parties, other than the technical providers named above.

Children

Prout Box asks for no name, e-mail or account, including from children. If you are under 15, ask a parent before publishing anything online.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, or deletion of, what you published by writing to contact@proutbox.com with your name in the ranking, the country and the date. The French version of this policy prevails in case of difference.
