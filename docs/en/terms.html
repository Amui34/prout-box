In one sentence: Prout Box is a free game made for laughs. Publish toots, not insults, and respect the other players.

Acceptance

By installing or using Prout Box you accept these terms and the privacy policy. If you are under 15, ask a parent before publishing anything online.

What you may do

  • Play with the box, record your own sounds and keep them on your phone.
  • Publish a recording in your country's public ranking, rate others, report them, remove yours.
  • Create or join private groups with a code, send recordings there and play the challenges.

What you agree not to do

  • Publish a recording that is not a toot: speech, insults, hateful content, sexual content, the voice of an identifiable person without their consent, protected music.
  • Choose a name or nickname that is insulting, discriminatory or misleading.
  • Cheat on votes, get around publication limits, disrupt the service or use it for commercial purposes.

Published content and moderation

What you publish remains yours. By publishing, you grant us a non-exclusive right to store it, distribute it to other players and let it be played in the app, worldwide, for as long as it is online. You can remove a recording at any time from the app.

Every recording published in the public ranking is checked before it appears: an automated check refuses voices and empty recordings, and doubtful cases are listened to by a moderator. Every entry can then be reported with one gesture. An entry reported several times is hidden automatically; in a group, one report hides it immediately. We may remove any content or block any device that does not respect these terms, without notice.

No account, no warranty

Prout Box works without an account. Your published recordings are tied to an anonymous identifier on your device: if you change phone or uninstall the app, you will no longer be able to remove them yourself (write to us and we will). The service is provided “as is”, free of charge; we may change or stop it, and we guarantee neither its availability nor the preservation of content.
